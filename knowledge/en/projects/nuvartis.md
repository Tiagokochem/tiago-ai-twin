## Overview

Nuvartis is my own product, built and maintained by me, live at nuvartis.com. It's a complete e-commerce platform to sell across multiple channels from a single dashboard.

## Features

- Integration with the Mercado Livre, Magalu and Shopee marketplaces.
- Real-time shipping quotes with Correios, Melhor Envio and carriers.
- Optimized checkout and integrated payment methods.

## What it shows

Integrating multiple external APIs (marketplaces, shipping and payments) in a real product in production, combining my e-commerce business background with software engineering.
