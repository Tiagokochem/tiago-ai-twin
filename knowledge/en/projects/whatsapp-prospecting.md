## Problem

Prospecting on WhatsApp usually takes hours of building automation flows. Most tools hand you an empty builder and leave the heavy lifting to the user.

## Solution

A ready-made operation: the user picks a niche, configures the offer and connects WhatsApp. The system uses per-niche playbooks, sends queued outreach and follow-ups, respects business hours and holidays, answers questions about pricing, scheduling and objections, and hands hot leads over to a human.

## My role

It's my own product, currently in validation. I was responsible for the product, the multi-tenant architecture, the Evolution API integration, the Free and Pro plans with Mercado Pago, the landing page and the admin dashboard.

## Stack

Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Evolution API, HTMX, Alpine.js and Docker.
