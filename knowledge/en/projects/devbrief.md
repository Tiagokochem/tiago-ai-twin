## Overview

DevBrief is a productivity SaaS I built and maintain, available at devbrief.online. It automates daily logs and standups for tech teams, with AI-generated summaries and subscription plans.

## Technical highlights

- Built with React and Node.js.
- Installable app (PWA) that also works offline.
- AI-generated summaries from daily entries.
- Subscription plans.
